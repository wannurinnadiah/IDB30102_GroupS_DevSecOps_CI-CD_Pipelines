# 2.4 Summary Table of Related Studies

| Theme. | Key Studies | Methods | Key Findings. | Limitations / Research Gaps. |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Shift-Left Security Automation & Multi-Tool Vulnerability Scanning | Matteo Esposito (2024), Dinis Cruz (2023), Abderrahim Rida (2026) | Multi-tool security automation (SAST/DAST/SCA); proxy CI/CD pipeline architecture; automated quality gate enforcement | Shift-left testing has enabled the teams to deliver their projects faster; integration of multiple tools has broadened their coverage; the proxy pipelines has achieved low rates of false positives (10-15%) and minimal scan costs. | Security scanning increases pipeline execution time by up to 41.8% in proxy architectures, with tool specific alert formats creating 30+% overhead in consolidated reporting. |
| Organizational Adoption, Developer Friction, and Quality Gate Barriers | De Vito et al. (2025); Ebert & Hochstein (2023); Cheenepalli et al. (2025) | Technology acceptance models (TAM & DOI), Security-First Agile playbooks as well as standardized declarative CI/CD pipelines and Identity Governance (IGA) frameworks. | 41% of the teams experience slowdowns due to the complexity of their toolchains; 38% due to team silos; only 12% of SMEs scan their code on every commit. Standardizing the governance of the teams has reduced errors by 87% and the MTTR from 97 minutes to 12 minutes. | Reliance on subjective professional judgment instead of objective build-time telemetry, with lack of evidence on commit latency reductions and developer bypass rates. |
| Deep Learning LLM and AI-Based Detection and Remediation | Kandadi et al. (2026); Kalwani (2026); Nagvekar (2025), Abdiukov (2024); Gangina (2024) | Hybrid Transformer GNN models, Random Forest meta-learners, RNN sequence analyzers; multi agent LLM using reinforcement learning | Observed 99.98% threat detection with less than one second latency(680 ms) using AI framework; 80% increase in successful deployment with multi agent LLM with 95% first attempt successful deployments. | Explainability, small or benchmark datasets, false positives and limited large-scale enterprise validation.
